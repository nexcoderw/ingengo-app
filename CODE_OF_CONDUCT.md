# Code of conduct

Ingengo welcomes contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds.
This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where
people represent the project.

- Treat people with respect. Critique the work and explain how it can improve.
- Welcome questions, different viewpoints, and constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy. Share another person's information only with their consent.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and threats are unacceptable.

If you experience or witness a violation, privately contact
[KAJUGA Daniels](https://github.com/kajugadaniels) through a contact channel listed
on the profile. Maintainers will review reports discreetly and may remove
content or restrict participation when appropriate. Security vulnerabilities
follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
